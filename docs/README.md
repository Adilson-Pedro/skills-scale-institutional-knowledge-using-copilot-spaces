## Brief summary of the OctoAcme project management process

OctoAcme follows a structured project lifecycle designed to turn ideas into delivered outcomes with clear ownership, regular communication, and continuous learning.

The process starts with project initiation, where stakeholders define the problem, objectives, scope, and success criteria, and identify the core team and roles. Once the need is clear, the team moves into planning, establishing timelines, milestones, dependencies, backlog priorities, and quality expectations.

During execution and tracking, work is delivered in iterative increments, coordinated by the project manager and product lead. The team monitors progress, manages dependencies, tracks risks, and keeps stakeholders informed through regular communication and status updates. Quality is ensured through testing, review, and acceptance criteria aligned with agreed outcomes.

As work nears completion, the team moves through release and deployment, validating the delivered solution, communicating launch plans, and transitioning the outcome into production or stakeholder use. After delivery, the team conducts retrospectives and continuous improvement reviews to capture lessons learned, identify follow-up actions, and refine the process for future work.

This lifecycle keeps the team aligned across planning, execution, risk management, delivery, and improvement so projects can be managed consistently and with measurable results.
