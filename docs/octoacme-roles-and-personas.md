# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction, authorize funding, and confirm that the project is delivering business value. They act as executive champions for the effort and help resolve cross-functional barriers.

### Responsibilities
- Define the strategic business case and expected outcomes
- Approve funding, scope priorities, and major investment decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate unresolved issues
- Align project delivery with business goals and portfolio priorities
- Support stakeholder alignment and executive communication

### Decision-Making Authority
- Approves the business case, funding, and major scope or priority changes
- Confirms go/no-go decisions for major phases or releases
- Resolves high-level trade-offs between business objectives and delivery constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to review status, risks, and escalations
- Aligns with Product Managers on business value, outcomes, and strategic priorities
- Collaborates with Developers and QA Leads to confirm that the delivered solution supports business goals and release readiness
- Provides support to stakeholders when decisions require executive backing or change in direction

### Goals
- Ensure the project remains aligned to strategic priorities
- Protect budget, value realization, and executive sponsorship
- Enable timely decisions to reduce project friction

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates and executive briefings
- Business case and milestone reviews
- High-level risk and dependency escalation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements and ensure the project delivers outcomes that meet stakeholder expectations. They connect business context with delivery teams and project governance.

### Responsibilities
- Gather, analyze, and document business and user requirements
- Clarify scope, acceptance criteria, and process impacts
- Identify business risks, dependencies, and stakeholder concerns
- Support solution design and process alignment with business needs
- Ensure requirements remain traceable to project objectives

### Decision-Making Authority
- Recommends requirement priorities and refinement decisions
- Validates that proposed solutions meet business intent and stakeholder needs
- Defines or clarifies acceptance criteria in coordination with Product Managers and stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to shape problem statements and prioritized requirements
- Works with Project Managers to align scope, dependencies, and milestone planning
- Supports Developers and QA Leads by turning business needs into testable requirements and acceptance criteria
- Helps Change Managers prepare communication and adoption efforts that match the target processes or workflows

### Goals
- Improve clarity and alignment between business goals and delivery outcomes
- Reduce ambiguity in requirements and reduce rework
- Ensure stakeholder expectations are reflected in the solution

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- User stories, process maps, and acceptance criteria reviews
- Cross-functional planning and signoff discussions

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define quality standards, coordinate validation activities, and ensure project deliverables meet agreed expectations before release. They partner closely with delivery teams to reduce defects and risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards, testing strategy, and review processes
- Coordinate functional, regression, and release validation activities
- Monitor defects, trends, and risk areas across the project
- Validate that deliverables meet requirements and acceptance criteria
- Support continuous improvement of quality practices

### Decision-Making Authority
- Recommends release readiness based on quality evidence and risk assessment
- Determines when testing is sufficient for a milestone or release gate
- Escalates quality risks that may affect scope, schedule, or stakeholder confidence

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to review testability, defect triage, and remediation planning
- Aligns with Product Managers and Business Analysts on acceptance criteria and expected outcomes
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release readiness, testing schedules, and cross-team dependencies
- Supports Change Managers by validating that solution changes are ready for rollout and user adoption

### Goals
- Minimize defects and quality-related delivery risk
- Build confidence in solution readiness and stakeholder trust
- Promote a shared understanding of quality expectations across teams

### Typical Communication
- Test planning and defect review meetings
- Quality dashboards and release readiness reports
- Requirements traceability and test result reviews

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers support adoption of project outcomes by helping stakeholders understand, accept, and use the new process, system, or capability. They focus on communication, training, and organizational readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Assess change impacts on people, teams, and processes
- Develop communication plans and adoption strategies
- Coordinate training, enablement, and stakeholder support activities
- Identify resistance, concerns, and readiness gaps
- Measure adoption and reinforce sustained change behavior

### Decision-Making Authority
- Recommends communication and adoption strategies for affected stakeholders
- Determines training priorities and readiness checkpoints for rollout
- Escalates organizational resistance that may affect project success

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinates with Project Managers on rollout timing, milestones, and stakeholder engagement plans
- Works with Product Managers and Business Analysts to align adoption efforts with business goals and user needs
- Partners with Developers and QA Leads to confirm that changes are ready for user release and support workflows
- Supports Sponsors by helping executive stakeholders communicate the value and impact of the change

### Goals
- Improve adoption of project outcomes and reduce resistance
- Increase stakeholder confidence in new ways of working
- Support sustained change beyond the initial implementation

### Typical Communication
- Change impact assessments and communication plans
- Training sessions, FAQs, and stakeholder briefings
- Adoption tracking and readiness reviews
